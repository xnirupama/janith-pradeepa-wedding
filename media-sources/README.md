# Original invitation videos

These are the supplied source videos, kept unchanged for future editing. They are outside `public` and excluded from Vercel uploads.

`video-inventory.json` records ffprobe dimensions, duration, frame rate, audio, bytes, the actual scene and section assignment. Every source was inspected at 1, 5 and 9 seconds; contact sheets are in ignored `artifacts/qa/video-inspection/`.

Published silent derivatives and posters are generated under `public/assets/*/optimized/` by the preparation scripts. See the project ASSETS.md and INVITATION-UPGRADE.md for regeneration and measured results.
